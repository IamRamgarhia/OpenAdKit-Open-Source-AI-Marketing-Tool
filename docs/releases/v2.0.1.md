## What's new in OpenAdKit 2.0.1

A quality release on top of 2.0: every one of the 30 AI tools was run end to
end through the MCP server, scored, and fixed where the prompt pushed a model
toward made-up or wrong output.

**More honest output**
- Prompts no longer state unsourced statistics as fact, and only ask for social
  proof, data or named customers that your Brand Brain actually contains.
- CTR, conversion rate, cost per click, cost per conversion, ad-fatigue decay
  and bid-strategy feasibility are now calculated by the app from your numbers
  instead of by the AI.
- Enhanced CPC is no longer recommended (Google retired it for Search); bid and
  keyword tools share one set of thresholds.
- Budget planner: break-even CPA can use your gross margin.

**Fixes**
- MCP: the Shopping, TikTok Branded Hashtag Challenge and Hashtags tools lost
  their own `brand` / `language` fields to shared options. Shared options are
  now `client` and `output_language` (update any saved MCP calls).
- Budget Waste now always runs its audit (with a warning when conversion
  tracking isn't confirmed) instead of returning an empty result.
- X / Twitter generator rewritten: per-format specs, 280-character posts with
  links counted as 23, optional landing URL and audience location.
- Platform rules corrected: Instagram's 5-hashtag cap, Display button length,
  YouTube 3-second hook, TikTok flagged as unavailable in India.
- More platform character limits are checked automatically (Display, X,
  TikTok, Shopping, Campaign Kit, Quality Score headlines).
- Adding a client by hand needs only the business name; email subject runs
  are filed under "email"; no landing-page flash for returning users.

Thanks to everyone testing the 2.0 builds.
